Trainee Programme

Start here with everything you need in place.

Our trainee programme takes people at the beginning of their careers and gives them the ground to stand on — not just the training, but the practical support that makes relocating for a first job possible.

  • Hostel accommodation Company-provided residential facilities close to the plant.
  • Daily transport Dedicated transport between accommodation and the units.
  • Structured training Hands-on rotation across foundry, machining and quality.
  • Mentorship Paired with senior engineers who have come up the same route.

Employee Welfare

A fund that's there when it's needed.

Shivalik maintains a standing welfare corpus for employees facing emergencies. It exists so that a medical crisis or a family emergency doesn't become a financial one — support is available quickly, without a lengthy process.

  • Emergency corpus A maintained fund available to employees in urgent need.
  • Medical support Assistance for employees and their immediate families.
  • Safety first ISO 45001-certified operations across every unit.
