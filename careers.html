Trainee Programme

Start here with everything you need in place.

At its core, Shivalik's trainee framework is built on one simple belief: talent that is hired locally, trained structurally and retained deliberately costs less and delivers more than talent
chased externally.
Rather than treating trainees as a transactional, revolving-door workforce, the program treats them as a long-term investment — one that needs to be protected (through contracts), nurtured (through structured training, mentorship, and cross-functional exposure) and grown (through defined promotion
and career paths).
The underlying philosophy shifts HR's role from reactive hiring to proactive workforce engineering — building a self-sustaining talent pipeline from campus/institute to shop floor to leadership, while safeguarding the company's training investment through transparency and legal accountability at every step.

  • Investment Protection through Structured Contracts Structured service agreements (₹5,00,000 for Management Trainees / ₹1,00,000 for Operators) with 3-year and 5-year lock-ins.
  • Retention-First Hiring Design Tier-based local hiring strategy combined with institute-based hiring (IIM/IIT/ITI/Polytechnic).
  • Transparency as a Non-Negotiable Value Transparent NAPS/NATS communication policy with signed candidate declarations.
  • End-to-End Structured Development Structured 6-month training program with cross-functional rotation and mentor-mentee system.
  • Clear Growth & Welfare Framework to Sustain Retention Defined 5-year growth path with performance-based promotions and employee welfare initiatives.

Employee Welfare

A fund that's there when it's needed.

The scheme proposed as "SEIL Parivar Suraksha Yojana" is built on the idea that an employer's responsibility extends beyond the wage into the family's critical moments: illness, death, marriage, childbirth, education and disaster.
By formalizing this support into a governed, budgeted and time-bound scheme (Shivalik Sahayog Scheme) rather than leaving it to ad-hoc discretion, the company converts goodwill into a structured system of trust. The hilosophy rests on treating the employee as part of a "Parivar" (family) - where support during hardship is not charity but an earned entitlement tied to tenure and need, governed transparently through a multi-tier committee structure so decisions are fair, fast and financially controlled.
Ultimately, it is designed to convert welfare spending into measurable business value: engagement, loyalty, retention and employer brand.

  • Structured 3-Tier Governance (CFT) Department → Plant → Management Welfare Committees, each with defined composition, authority limits and decision timelines (3/7/10 days).
  • Amount-Based Approval Matrix Up to ₹25,000 (Department), ₹25,001–₹1,00,000 (Plant), above ₹1,00,000 (Supreme Committee).
  • Comprehensive Coverage Areas Medical emergencies, death, marriage, education, maternity and natural calamity/house damage, each with defined assistance slabs.
  • Emergency Fast-Track Protocol HR + Plant Head can approve immediate relief up to ₹50,000 within 24 hours for life-threatening/accident/death cases, with post-facto committee ratification.
  • Budgeted & Monitored Fund (₹50,00,000 for FY 2026-27) Applicable to permanent employees (Supervisor & below, min. 1 year service), with monthly review, misuse checks and MIS tracking for accountability.
