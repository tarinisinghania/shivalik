The beginning

2007

Shivalik began at the heart of India's steel industry, with a simple belief: a casting made right the first time is worth more than a hundred explanations later.

Built to scale

Nearly two decades on

Across four manufacturing units, our foundries produce more than 111,600 metric tonnes of castings each year. But the number we value most isn't one you'll find in a brochure — it's the reliability of every casting once it leaves our foundry.

The same promise

Every order, every market

Every wheel hub destined for a Volvo Eicher assembly line, every housing shipped to Webb Wheel in the United States, carries the promise we made to our very first customer: it will fit, it will perform, and it will last.

The people behind it

1,500 strong

Design engineers who simulate every mould before production begins, metallurgists who refine every melt, and operators whose experience tells them — often by sound alone — when something isn't right. Precision here isn't a process; it's a habit.

What comes next

2026 and beyond

We're investing in cleaner manufacturing, solar-powered operations, advanced automation and ever tighter tolerances. Technology evolves and expectations rise — the reason we build stays the same.

Why it matters

Since 2007

Every casting leaves our foundry with a responsibility. Somewhere ahead, a vehicle is carrying people, products and livelihoods — and it depends on the quality of what we build.

MD's Message

At Shivalik, we believe that lasting success is built on integrity, commitment, excellence and professionalism.

Since our beginning, we have remained focused on understanding our customers' needs and delivering precision-engineered castings with consistency, reliability and care.

The automotive industry is changing rapidly. New technologies, higher standards and growing expectations are reshaping the way we manufacture. Our strength lies in our ability to evolve with that change while remaining grounded in the principles that have guided us from the start.

As India continues to emerge as a global manufacturing hub, Shivalik is proud to contribute to that progress. We continue to invest in technology, people and sustainable manufacturing to build stronger capabilities for the future.

We look forward to building lasting partnerships and creating greater value for our customers, our people and the industries we serve.
