our story

We believe in earning trust through
our actions and always strive for outstanding results for
our customers and partners.

Shivalik began in 2007, at the heart of India's steel industry, with a simple belief: a casting made right the first time is worth more than a hundred explanations later.

Nearly two decades later, that belief continues to shape everything we do. Across four manufacturing units, our foundries produce more than 111,600 metric tonnes of castings each year. But the number we value most isn't one you'll find in a brochure, it's the reliability of every casting once it leaves our foundry.

Every wheel hub destined for a Volvo Eicher assembly line, every housing shipped to Webb Wheel in the United States, carries the same promise we made to our very first customer: it will fit, it will perform, and it will last. That promise is upheld by more than 1,500 people, design engineers who simulate every mould before production begins, metallurgists who refine every melt, and operators whose experience tells them, often by sound alone, when something isn't perfect. Precision here isn't a process; it's a habit built over years of craftsmanship. As we grow, we're investing in the future through cleaner manufacturing, solar-powered operations, advanced automation and ever tighter tolerances. Technology evolves, industries change, and expectations rise but the reason we build remains the same.

Every casting leaves our foundry with a responsibility. Because somewhere ahead, a vehicle is carrying people, products and livelihoods and it depends on the quality of what we build. That responsibility has defined Shivalik since 2007, and it continues to shape everything we do today.
