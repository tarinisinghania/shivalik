03 — Sustainability

A greener foundry, built into how we manufacture.

Sustainability isn't bolted on after the fact — it's designed into the process, from captive solar power to closed-loop water and near-total material recovery.

Road to Carbon Neutrality

Cut carbon intensity at 1.44 tCO2e/lmt, targeting a further 50% reduction by 2026.

Setting up a 55 MW solar plant by 2026.

Targeting 50% GHG emission reduction by 2026 (from 2023 baseline).

By 2027: 60% green energy and carbon neutrality, aligned with the Paris Agreement.

Emissions & Air Quality Compliance

  • FY 2025: GHG emissions per ton of cast maintained below the 1.52 target.
  • Air Quality Index assays consistently meet regulatory thresholds — zero violations in the last 3 years.
  • High-efficiency fume extraction units reducing particulate emissions by 70%.
  • Full compliance ensured through real-time emission monitoring.

Verified for Transparency

Independently verified — every Shivalik figure stands for trust, accuracy and global accountability.

Water Conservation & Reuse

  • Rainwater harvesting system with 2.84 million KL annual capacity.
  • Closed-loop cooling reduces fresh water demand by 40%.
  • Water reuse treats & recycles over 80% of process wastewater.
  • Together conserving roughly 6 million litres of municipal water each year.

Waste Recycling & Circular Economy

  • Near 100% recycling rates for sand & metal waste.
  • Sand waste recycling rate: 98% (FY 2024).
  • Metal waste recycling rate: 99%.
  • Reusable packaging eliminating over 500 kg of plastic waste per annum.

Sustainability at a Glance

  • Minimise environmental impact through proactive waste management and emission control.
  • 55 MW solar power project across 125 acres, commencing March 2026.
  • Targeting 80% renewable energy utilisation.
  • Metal waste reuse, sand recycling and eco-friendly packaging.
