Our Transformation

Three pillars carrying Shivalik into its
next decade.

01 — Technology

Automation, simulation and data on the foundry floor.

We're rebuilding how a casting comes to life. Every mould is simulated before a grain of sand is packed; robotic cells handle fettling and machining with repeatable precision; and live process data lets us catch a deviation before it becomes a defect. Technology here isn't a showpiece — it's how we hold quality steady at scale, shorten lead times, and take on parts that older methods simply couldn't deliver.

02 — Human

The people who know, by sound, when something isn't right.

Machines don't run a foundry — people do. Our transformation invests as much in the fifteen hundred hands behind every casting as in the equipment they operate: continuous training, safer workspaces, and career paths that grow with the company. From design engineers to floor operators, we're building a workforce that treats precision as a habit and takes ownership of what leaves the gate.

03 — Sustainability

A greener foundry, built into how we manufacture.

Sustainability isn't bolted on after the fact — it's designed into the process. Our captive solar plant powers a growing share of operations, cutting grid dependence and lowering the carbon footprint of every part we cast. Cleaner melting, sand reclamation and tighter energy control mean we can grow output without growing our impact, delivering castings our customers can stand behind on every front.
