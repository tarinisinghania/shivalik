Streamlined end-to-end manufacturing: from design to delivery.

our manufacturing

Four units.
One standard.

Shivalik runs a vertically integrated manufacturing base across four operational units in and around Bhilai : grey and SG iron foundries, a dedicated machine shop, and specialised lines feeding commercial vehicle, agricultural, railway and off-highway customers. Every process from melt to despatch happens under our own roof, which is how we hold quality steady at scale. Three more units are coming online through 2026, adding automated fettling, flaskless moulding and captive solar power.

  8. 08

    Testing Facilities

    Comprehensive labs for chemical, dimensional and non-destructive testing verify every batch against specification before despatch.

    CapabilityChemical · Dimensional · NDT
    • Fully integrated sand and metal labs ensure comprehensive quality control.
    • Equipped with spectrometer for precise composition analysis.
    • Leak testing detects casting imperfections and ensures integrity.
    • Three Coordinate Measuring Machines (CMMs) for accurate dimensional inspection.
    • Ultrasonic Testing (UT meter) identifies internal flaws nondestructively.
    • 3D scanner, profile projector and contour tracer optimize geometry and surface profile assessments.

    Robust testing facilities guarantee defect-free castings with consistent quality.

  9. 09

    Material Handling & Logistics

    Advanced handling equipment and engineered packing ensure castings reach customers safely and on schedule, anywhere in the world.

    CapabilityEngineered packing · Global despatch
    • 3-ton forklifts for versatile on-site movement.
    • 7.5/5-ton EOT cranes with magnets for raw material and liquid metal handling.
    • 0.5-ton XY rail crane system in fettling area for precise handling.
    • JCB earth mover for waste disposal.
    • Tractors with trolleys for in-house transport.
    • 360-meter Vinar overhead cooling conveyor linking foundry to fettling with 250 hangers.
    • Strict packing standards aligned to customer specs to prevent damage and rejections.
    • Special shrink-wrap packing machines for quality, cost-effective packaging.
    • Dot matrix QR code and laser marking machines for traceability and identification.

    Integrated logistics and advanced equipment ensure efficient, safe, & quality-assured delivery.
