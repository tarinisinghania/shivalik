Streamlined end-to-end manufacturing: from design to delivery.

our manufacturing

Four units.
One standard.

Shivalik runs a vertically integrated manufacturing base across four operational units in and around Bhilai : grey and SG iron foundries, a dedicated machine shop, and specialised lines feeding commercial vehicle, agricultural, railway and off-highway customers. Every process from melt to despatch happens under our own roof, which is how we hold quality steady at scale. Three more units are coming online through 2026, adding automated fettling, flaskless moulding and captive solar power.

Technical Details

From design to delivery, in-house.

A streamlined, end-to-end manufacturing chain — every stage owned, controlled and quality-checked under one operation.

  1. 01

    Design & R&D

    Innovative designs developed with CAD/CAM and full casting simulation, validating mould filling, solidification and stress behaviour before any metal is poured.

    CapabilityCAD/CAM · Simulation · Tailored tooling
  2. 02

    Furnace & Melting

    Precision melting on induction and cupola furnaces, with tight control of chemistry and temperature to hold material consistency across every heat.

    CapabilityInduction · Cupola · Spectro control
  3. 03

    Sand Plant

    Automated sand mixing and reclamation with continuous property control, ensuring optimal mould strength and repeatable surface finish.

    CapabilityAutomated mixing · Reclamation
  4. 04

    Core Making

    Automated cold-box and shell core shooters produce complex internal geometries with dimensional accuracy for intricate castings.

    CapabilityCold-box · Shell · Complex cores
  5. 05

    Moulding Line

    High-pressure flaskless moulding lines deliver consistent compaction and high-volume output without sacrificing dimensional control.

    CapabilityFlaskless · High-pressure · High volume
  6. 06

    Fettling & Painting

    In-house finishing with shot blasting, grinding and automated spray painting, delivering castings ready for assembly straight off the line.

    CapabilityShot blasting · Auto spray paint
  7. 07

    Machine Shop

    CNC, VMC and special-purpose machines handle precision machining to final tolerances, keeping the full value chain under one roof.

    CapabilityCNC · VMC · SPM machining
  8. 08

    Testing Facilities

    Comprehensive labs for chemical, dimensional and non-destructive testing verify every batch against specification before despatch.

    CapabilityChemical · Dimensional · NDT
  9. 09

    Material Handling & Logistics

    Advanced handling equipment and engineered packing ensure castings reach customers safely and on schedule, anywhere in the world.

    CapabilityEngineered packing · Global despatch
