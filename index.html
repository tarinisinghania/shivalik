ABOUT SHIVALIK

With the largest manufacturing footprint, quality, scalability & precision in the industry, Shivalik sets the benchmark for every product we create.

Shivalik Engineering Industries Limited is a trusted manufacturing partner for precision- engineered cast and machined components, serving leading OEMs and industrial customers across global markets. Backed by vertically integrated operations, advanced manufacturing technologies, and deep engineering expertise, the company delivers high-quality solutions for commercial vehicles, agricultural equipment, off-highway machinery, railways, and infrastructure applications. With a diversified product portfolio, large-scale manufacturing capabilities, and a commitment to operational excellence, Shivalik continues to drive performance, reliability, and value for customers worldwide.
